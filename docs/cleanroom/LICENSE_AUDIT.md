Gradle wrapper tooling is Apache-2.0; preserve Gradle attribution/license with distribution.

Its complete upstream license text is retained at `third_party_licenses/NewPipeExtractor-GPL-3.0.txt`; dependency source: [TeamNewPipe/NewPipeExtractor](https://github.com/TeamNewPipe/NewPipeExtractor/tree/v0.26.5).
